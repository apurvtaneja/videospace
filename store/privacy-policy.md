# VideoSpace Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

VideoSpace is a Chrome extension that moves a fullscreen YouTube video's tab into its own macOS Space and returns it to its window afterwards.

**Data collection.** VideoSpace does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

**Local data.** While a video is fullscreen, VideoSpace keeps a temporary record of the tab's original window, position, pinned state and tab group so it can restore the tab. This record is held in Chrome's in-memory session storage (`chrome.storage.session`). It never leaves your device, it is deleted when the tab is restored, and Chrome clears it when the browser closes.

**Page access.** VideoSpace runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`, and only to detect when the video player requests fullscreen. It does not read, store or modify page content.
